Ratatui ecosystem libraries
===========================
Checked 2026-09-09 against this project: ratatui 0.30.2, crossterm 0.29.0.
Versions are what crates.io served that day. All entries below resolve
cleanly on ratatui 0.30.2 unless noted.

COMPATIBILITY WARNING
---------------------
ratatui 0.30 split the crate into ratatui-core / ratatui-widgets, so a
third-party widget needs a release that targets 0.30. If it doesn't, cargo
quietly resolves TWO copies of ratatui and the widget's types won't unify
with your Buffer.

  After adding any widget crate, run:  cargo tree -i ratatui
  If it answers "specification `ratatui` is ambiguous", you have two
  versions and it will not work.

  KNOWN BAD: tui-textarea 0.7.0  -- pins ratatui ^0.29. Do not use on 0.30.
